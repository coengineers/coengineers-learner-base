## The offer and the app

**Windowsill to Plate** is a chilli-only digital membership for UK beginners: one recipe, one short video and one small job a week, with a community post. Founding membership is £8 a month (Testing), for 10 to 20 places, paid through Gumroad. Chilli's app has Home, Recipes, Videos and Community, and lives at chilli.coengineers.ai. Chilli's look is the CoEngineers look with its own wordmark and two small colours, leaf green and chilli red. The people: Priya S. (tried twice on a Leeds windowsill, got nothing), Tom H. ## What happened

**The journey:** "This week". A member opens the app and sees the week's job, recipe and video on Home.

- **The done test (decided before building):** a member finds the job without help, in two taps or fewer.
- **The plan:** four steps: sign in, Home shows the week's job, tap for the recipe, tap for the video. The recipe and video are practice content.
- **The check:** on a phone, four taps with a tick beside each. It matched the done test.
- **Priya S. tried it** on her phone and found the job first time (Simulated for the course).
