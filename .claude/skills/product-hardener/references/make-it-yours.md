# Make it yours: product hardener

What a founder may change about this skill. Their kept changes live in `skills/product-hardener.md` in their second brain, which the skill reads first. Changes cannot touch the truth, save or time rules.

## What members would mind most
Your own words for what must never leak or be lost. It moves the ranking.

## Extra areas
Anything you also want looked at, for example a certain page. Security always comes first.

## Dates
How far ahead a "later" fix is dated: two weeks by default.

## Wording
How the page words a risk: plain, in terms of what a member would notice.
