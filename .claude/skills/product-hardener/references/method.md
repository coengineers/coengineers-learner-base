# Method: looking for weak spots

Read this when you look at an area.

## Sign-in

Try five wrong passwords: is there a limit or a delay? Is email confirmation on? Does password recovery work, and does its link expire? Does a signed-out person always get the join page and never a broken screen?

## Who sees what

Make four practice people: signed out, signed in but not a member, a second member, and a member who then cancels. Try each on every page and every doorway action, including guessing another member's address for a post or a photo. Each is refused with a plain message and nothing leaks (not a name, not a title).

## Where things are kept

Members' details live only in the database, in the agreed region. Keys are in the secret settings where the app lives, not in the code, not in logs, not in the second brain. Run the secret scan and the unsafe-package scan. Check the error log holds no member details. Check who can open the database and online-home accounts: the founder only, with two-step sign-in on.

## Payment and access in step

A cancelled or refunded purchase removes access on the next visit. The same sale twice lets nobody in twice. A message that does not really come from the shop is refused. Founding members added by hand: is there a list, and does removing someone remove access?

## Ranking

For each weak spot: how many members it could hurt, how badly (their details seen, their access lost, money wrong, a screen looks odd), and how likely. Highest harm first. Cosmetic problems come last however easy they are.

## Rechecking

After a fix, be the member it would have hurt. Say what they now see. Repeat the earlier journeys once more.
