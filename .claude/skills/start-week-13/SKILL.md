# Week 13 lab: Plan version 1

Read `references/lab-rules.md` first and follow its eight steps. This page gives the week's details. The build is done by the `product-prd-builder` skill. About 30 minutes.

## Before you start

Find the founder's second brain (as the lab rules say). Read `offer/OFFER.md`, the Week 12 decision, `customers/` conversations, `product/` test notes and `product/app-map.md` (what the base app already does). If there is no app map yet, use the base app's standard list (sign-in, a profile, private files, the Claude doorway) and say so in one line; do not ask the founder what it does. If there is no offer, send them to the Week 12 lab first.

**Bring:** last week's decision, the names of the people who said yes, and access to their own Supabase and Cloudflare accounts prepared in Week 12 Do.

Start from learner-owned accounts. Confirm they can sign in to both dashboards and own them; never say CoEngineers made them. If either is missing, give the Week 12 Do account-preparation route and offer the accounts support session. Planning can continue with setup marked not yet, but do not claim an app has been connected.

For the £1.50 activity, offer a promise to pay or a bank transfer arranged by the learner with the person directly. Record which route they choose and the real outcome: promised, reported paid, declined or not yet. Save it in the Week 13 weekly note under "Decisions" and in this week's What happened note (/did-it asks for it). A promise is not a payment. Never request bank details in chat or initiate a transfer. Say "Real payment setup is in Week 17; you do not need a payment page for this activity."

## Check-in

Read the Week 12 decision note and its first-step task. If the offer changed, work from the new one.

From the Week 12 What happened section and weekly note: the call they made, what they did next, and whether each app account (Supabase, Cloudflare) is ready or not yet.

**Open with last week's call, before any planning.** Your first reply states the call in one line and what it means for today, then asks them to confirm:
- **Go:** the plan is built from the offer as it stands.
- **Retest:** the retest's answer comes first. If it is in, say what it showed. If not, say the plan will rest on an offer still being tested, and ask whether to plan now or after the answer.
- **Pivot:** the plan is for the new direction, and only from what the three conversations said. If they have not happened, say so, and ask whether to plan now or wait.
- **Stop:** there is nothing to plan. Do not start the lab; say what the Week 12 lab said about the two doors.
- **No decision saved:** send them to the Week 12 lab first.
Never go straight to drafts.

## Decisions (two rounds at most)

1. **Who uses version 1 first, and the one thing a member must be able to do in their first week.** Draft both from the offer's promise.
2. **The three trays.** The builder drops each line of the offer into one tray: already in the base app, new in version 1, or done by hand for now. The founder moves lines, keeps at most three new features and says what waits and why.
3. **The £1.50 route.** Promise to pay or a bank transfer the learner arranges with the person directly. Draft the route from what the person said; the founder confirms it in their own words.

## Artifact

The Week 13 Plan page (the builder makes it): the offer's lines in the three trays, up to three features each with a done test a member could check, what waits and why. In the founder's brand, readable on a phone.

## Criteria

- Every item traces to the offer.
- The first version fits in three features.
- Each feature has a done test a member could check.
- What waits is written down with why.

## Records

- `product/PRD.md` (the Week 13 Plan, kept up to date; each feature's done test is a line inside this Plan, not a separate document)
- `weekly/YYYY-MM-DD-week-13-plan.md`, including each account's ready or not yet and the £1.50 route with its outcome

## Tasks

- "Show the plan to <name>": product, linked to their contact, due before your next lab. Someone who fits the members you want. If the £1.50 activity is agreed, include the chosen promise or learner-arranged bank-transfer route and record the outcome without treating a promise as money received.

## Close

When you have done it, type /did-it.
