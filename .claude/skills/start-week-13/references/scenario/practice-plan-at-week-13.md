# Chilli Community at Week 13: the plan for version 1

All of this is Chilli's, for practice only. Results are marked "Simulated for the course". Never save it to the founder's second brain or task list.

## The offer and the app

**Windowsill to Plate** is a chilli-only digital membership for UK beginners: one recipe, one short video and one small job a week, with a community post. Founding membership is £8 a month (Testing), for 10 to 20 places, paid through Gumroad. Chilli's app has Home, Recipes, Videos and Community, and lives at chilli.coengineers.ai. Chilli's look is the CoEngineers look with its own business name as a logo and two small colours, leaf green and chilli red. The people: Priya S. (tried twice on a Leeds windowsill, got nothing), Tom H. (bought a garden-centre plant, the flowers dropped), Jo W. (grows herbs, first-time tester).

## What happened

Each line of Chilli's offer dropped into one of three trays.

- **Already in the base app:** sign-in and each member's profile.
- **New in version 1 (three features):** "This week" (Home shows the week's job, recipe and video), "The weekly post" (a member shares a photo of their plant, other members see it) and "Payments and access" (a Gumroad purchase adds a member, a cancelled one removes access).
- **By hand for now:** founding members added by hand; payments stay a Gumroad link.
- **Waits, with why:** a searchable recipe library (nobody has asked for it yet) and reminders by email (the pilot will show whether they are needed).

**A done test John wrote for the first feature:** "A member opens Home and sees this week's job, recipe and video without tapping anywhere else." He showed the plan to Aisha K., who runs a village gardening group.
