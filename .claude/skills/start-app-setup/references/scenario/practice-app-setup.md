## The offer and the app

**Windowsill to Plate** is a chilli-only digital membership for UK beginners: one recipe, one short video and one small job a week, with a community post. Founding membership is £8 a month (Testing), for 10 to 20 places, paid through Gumroad. Chilli's app has Home, Recipes, Videos and Community, and lives at chilli.coengineers.ai. Chilli's look is the CoEngineers look with its own wordmark and two small colours, leaf green and chilli red. The people: Priya S. (tried twice on a Leeds windowsill, got nothing), Tom H. ## What happened

Chilli's copy of the starter app, the place that keeps members' details and the place that puts the app online were set up in one sitting, with Claude joining them up in Claude Code, the building tool. John owned every account and typed his own secret codes into each site's own page.

- **Name and region:** Chilli Community, with the data kept in the UK because the members are there.
- **Accounts:** GitHub, the place for members' details and the place that puts the app online, each in John's name, each with his own password and two-step sign-in.
- **Result:** the starter app open on its test web address, John signed in as himself, a practice file added and removed.
- **The app map:** one page. What lives where (the app's copy on GitHub, the members' details in the database, the pages on the site that puts the app online), who owns each, what it costs today (nothing, on free plans), and what comes next: Home, Recipes, Videos and Community.
