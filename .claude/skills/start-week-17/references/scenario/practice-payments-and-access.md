## What happened

**The loop:** a Gumroad purchase adds a member, the member gets in and uses the app, and an ended purchase removes access under those rules.

- **By hand for now:** John adds founding members himself. Each is written down: who, the date, and the price agreed.
- **The payment page:** John opened Chilli's payment page on his phone. It opened, showed £8 a month and what members get. Nobody bought, cancelled or refunded anything.
- **Access:** a member who cancels keeps access to the end of the month they paid for; a refunded member loses it at once.
