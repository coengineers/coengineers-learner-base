# Method: running a small pilot

Read this when you size the pilot or review a round.

## Size and length

Small enough to look after by hand: 4 to 10 members for four to six weeks is usual. Fewer people, watched closely, teach more than many watched loosely. The end date is fixed when the pilot starts; extending it is a new decision.

## Measures

Two or three, each a number that is easy to get: members who opened the app that week, members who posted, members who are still paying. Say where each number comes from (the app's records, the shop's list, asking). If a number needs the app to record something, say so and check it does.

## One change per round

A round is a week. Read what happened, choose the single change that would help most members, make it, and see what changes. Two changes at once make it impossible to tell which helped.

## Asking members

A short question, the same each week, by the way they already use (a message, a post). Draft it in the founder's words. The founder sends it.

## Invitations

One task per person, linked to their contact. The founder sends each. Someone who said "not now" is asked again only if the founder chooses.

## Stopping early

Write the line in advance: for example, a member's private details seen by another, or access wrong for a paying member. That stops the pilot until fixed.
