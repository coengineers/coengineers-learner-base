# Chilli Community at Week 21: the pilot

All of this is Chilli's, for practice only. Results are marked "Simulated for the course". Never save it to the founder's second brain or task list.

## The offer and the app

**Windowsill to Plate** is a chilli-only digital membership for UK beginners: one recipe, one short video and one small job a week, with a community post. Founding membership is £8 a month (Testing), for 10 to 20 places, paid through Gumroad. Chilli's app has Home, Recipes, Videos and Community, and lives at chilli.coengineers.ai. Chilli's look is the CoEngineers look with its own wordmark and two small colours, leaf green and chilli red. The people: Priya S. (tried twice on a Leeds windowsill, got nothing), Tom H. (bought a garden-centre plant, the flowers dropped), Jo W. (grows herbs, first-time tester).

## What happened

**The plan:** six invited, a six-week pilot with an end date, £8 a month. Four joined (Priya S., Tom H., Jo W. and one more, Simulated for the course).

- **Weekly measures:** members who opened the app, members who posted, and one question asked of each member.
- **One change per round.** Week one: two members had not opened the app by Wednesday. The change: a Tuesday reminder.
- **The board:** a six-week strip with one check-in dot a week and one improvement arrow per round.
