# Week 21 lab: Open the pilot

Read `references/lab-rules.md` first and follow its eight steps. If the installed reference files cannot be opened, read the same files from the founder's approved app with `GitHub:get_file_contents`: `.claude/skills/start-week-21/references/lab-rules.md`, `save-rules.md` and `task-rules.md` in that same folder. Find the app from the founder's context or app map. Use this read-only fallback before asking the founder to locate files. If neither copy can be read, name the missing rules and stop before any write. This page gives the week's details. The plan and the page are made by the `pilot-reviewer` skill. About 30 minutes.

## Before you start

Find the founder's second brain (`GitHub:search_repositories`, query `user:@me second-brain`). Read `product/PRD.md`, the launch record, the weak-spots page, `offer/OFFER.md` and the asks from Week 11 (`offer/`). Read the contacts in their task list (the CoEngineers CRM) who said yes or not now. If the app is not online, send them to the Week 19 lab first.

A working free test address counts as online for planning the pilot. Do not require a bought web address or a launch record to make the plan. Keep every unresolved member-safety step visible, and keep invitations and the paid pilot waiting for those steps; a plan is not proof that anyone was invited or paid.

**Bring:** the names of up to six people to invite first. No names yet? They say so at the start.

## Check-in

Read the Week 20 "Fix <weak spot>" tasks. Anything open that could hurt a member is settled before anyone is invited.

From the Week 20 What happened section: the weak spots fixed and whether the recheck passed.

## Decisions (two rounds at most)

1. **Size and end date.** How many members and for how many weeks. Draft from the plan and the yeses.
2. **Measures, change rule and asking members.** Weekly measures (draft three), one change per round at most, and how they will ask members what they think.

## Artifact

What they get is the Pilot page (the builder makes it): the plan, the people, a strip of weeks with one check-in each, and one change per round. In their brand, on a phone. Nothing is sent to anyone.

## Criteria

- A size and an end date.
- What you will measure each week.
- One change per round at most.
- How you will ask members what they think.

## Records

Before any GitHub write, read `references/save-rules.md` and follow its five steps. Prepare and read back the notes on a new branch, then give the named comparison link with the verified branch, title and body. The founder puts the change forward there and saves it with the two green buttons. Never call `GitHub:create_pull_request`, refer to a form or Submit, or describe an already opened change as still needing to be put forward. The notes are prepared until the founder approves and the saved notes are read back. Read the saved notes before making tasks, and never repeat successful task writes on a retry.

## Tasks

- "Invite <name> to the pilot": sales, one per person, linked to their contact, due before your next lab. The founder sends each invitation.
- "Pilot check-in": a weekly task, category product, one for each week of the pilot, on a day they choose.

## Close

They open each "Invite <name> to the pilot" task in the task list and send the invitation themselves. Say which pilot check-ins fall before the next lab. Bring the app on their phone to the End of Ship live session. When the invitations are sent, type /did-it.

Offer the material in `references/scenario/` as practice, clearly labelled, without saving it.
