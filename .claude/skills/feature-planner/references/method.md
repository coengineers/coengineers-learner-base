# Method: sizing a feature

Read this when you draft the steps.

## The base app's shape

The pages are what members see. The doorway checks who is asking and does only what that person may. Each person's details and files are kept so only they can read them. A new feature adds pages, doorway actions and stored things, in that order, and reuses sign-in, profiles and private files. Anything shared between members is new work and needs its own rules for who can see it.

## A good step

- One thing a person can try on the test address.
- Small: about a quarter of an hour of building.
- Stored things come before the pages that show them; the page comes before the extras.
- The first step should already show something.

## Privacy

Anything private or shared gets two tests written into the plan: a second member tries to see or change it and cannot, and a signed-out visitor gets nothing. Who is asking always comes from their sign-in, never from what a page says.

## Content

Practice content is marked "Practice" and removed when the real content arrives. Recipes, videos and photos are the founder's own. Nothing is generated to stand in for a real one.

## Money and access

The shop tells the app when someone buys or cancels. The app checks the message is really from the shop, lets the right person in once, and removes access when a purchase ends. Founding members added by hand are a written list the founder keeps, and the plan says who adds them and how. Nothing is bought: the checks use the shop's sample messages, and the founder only looks at the payment page. A member who cancels keeps access to the end of the month they paid for; a refunded member loses it at once.
