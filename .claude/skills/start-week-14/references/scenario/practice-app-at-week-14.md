## The offer and the app

**Windowsill to Plate** is a chilli-only digital membership for UK beginners: one recipe, one short video and one small job a week, with a community post. Founding membership is £8 a month (Testing), for 10 to 20 places, paid through Gumroad. Chilli's app has Home, Recipes, Videos and Community, and lives at chilli.coengineers.ai.
