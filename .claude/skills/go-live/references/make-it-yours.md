# Make it yours: go live

What a founder may change about this skill. Their kept changes live in `skills/go-live.md` in their second brain, which the skill reads first. Changes cannot touch the truth, save or time rules.

## Release name
How a release is named: "Version 1.0, <date>" by default.

## Who watches
Who looks at the error log and the usage each week, and when. Default: the founder, on their check-in day.

## The note to members
The drafted note if you go back: its tone and length.

## The doors
How you record the doors' date. It is always in your words.
