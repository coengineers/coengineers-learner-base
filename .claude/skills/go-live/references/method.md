# Method: putting the app live safely

Read this when you set up the live side or run the checks.

## Two addresses

The test address follows the main version. The real address changes only when a named release is made: a marked point on the main version, so it is always possible to say what members use and to return to it. Work can carry on after launch without members seeing half-finished changes.

## Keeping test and live apart

Same code, nothing else shared. Live has its own member data (on the free plans, the second project), its own keys held where the app lives online, and sign-in told the real address. Sign-in sending people back to the test address is the most common fault on launch day.

## The real address

Managed where the app lives: connect directly; the padlock follows in minutes. Held elsewhere: one setting to add there. Give the exact words, one step at a time, and wait until it answers securely.

## The checks (on the real address, with one made-up account)

- Live and loading, at phone and laptop size, from the named release.
- Sign-in: in, out, back in, on the real address.
- The joining route from the plan: for payments through the shop, a sample message in its published shape lets the made-up member in and a bad one is refused; for by-hand joining, check the list and removal.
- Private stays private: a signed-out visitor, a signed-in non-member and a second member each try something not theirs and are refused.
- Backups, costs, error log, going back: checked by looking.
- Privacy note and terms open from the sign-in page and footer, marked as drafts.

A check is Passed only when it ran and worked. Otherwise Not yet, Planned or Your step.

## Money

Live payment keys are marked in their first characters. Never use, copy or test one. Payments are checked in test mode with sample messages. Switching to live payments is the founder's own step.

## Backups

The free plans keep none automatically. A backup covers member data, files, settings and secret settings (kept separately). It counts only once restored somewhere safe and checked: people can sign in and see only their own things. Say what is in place, how often, where, and the last practice restore, or "Not yet tried".

## Costs

Read the free limits from the app's cost note and the real usage from each dashboard. Record numbers or "Not yet measured". At 70% look into it; at 90% pause anything non-essential. Never switch on a paid plan as a fix.

## Going back

After a later release, put the previous release back: minutes. After the first, show a short "back soon" page. Data is never undone by deleting: a bad change to stored data is fixed with a further change.

## Privacy note and terms

Plain first drafts from what the app really does: what it keeps, who sees it, where it is stored, how long, how to have it deleted; where payment details are held (with the shop). Business details from `business/` or the founder; a missing one shows "To be decided". Not legal advice; say so once.

## The landing page

It keeps its words, look and joining button; only its home changes. A temporary page's sign-ups do not travel: they stay with the old page for the founder to bring across. The old page stays up until the new one is checked.
