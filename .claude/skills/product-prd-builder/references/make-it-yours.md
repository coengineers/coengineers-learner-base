What a founder may change about this skill. Their kept changes live in `skills/product-prd-builder.md` in their second brain, which the skill reads first. Changes cannot touch the truth, save or time rules.

The names of the three trays and their order on the page.

What the Plan page shows first. It always shows the trays, the features and what waits.
