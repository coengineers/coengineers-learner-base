# Method: sorting the offer

Read this when you need what the base app gives, its limits or how to order features.

## What the base app already gives

Sign-in and password recovery; a profile (each person saves a display name); private files (upload, list, download, remove, visible only to the owner, small files only); a Claude doorway that has no product actions yet; works on phone and laptop; the founder's brand set in one place; checks on every change.

## Its limits (these raise open questions)

- Sized for a pilot of about 20 active people on free plans and about 200 MB of files in all. Videos will not fit: show them from where they are already hosted.
- Files are private to their owner. Anything members share with each other is new work.
- No payments, content, community, founder-only area, emails or notifications. Each is new work only if version 1 needs it.

## Ordering features

Easier first: showing what the founder made to signed-in members; saving one person's choices. Middle: members adding things others see, photos shared between members, counts that change as others act. Harder last: money and who gets in because of it; another company's service; anything on a timetable; a founder-only area members must never reach. Money and access almost always go third.

## Done tests

One sentence: start, do, see. Include, for the plan's later checks: a phone-sized screen, something going wrong, and (where anything is private or shared) a second member and a signed-out visitor trying to see it. For money: paid once means in once; not paid means not in; the same sale twice lets nobody in twice.

## Open questions

Where videos live; how members sign in on the real address; who adds founding members and when. Each with why it matters, who answers and before which feature.
